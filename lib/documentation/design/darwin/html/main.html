Welcome to iStation

iStation plays PS1 games on your Mac. A BIOS is the small program built into a console that starts it up. iStation runs yours, so your games start the way they do on your console.

It comes from your own console: iStation can’t provide one, and it expects a 512 KB file.

iStation never downloads a BIOS or games, and never sends anything off your Mac.
This version lists your disc images but can’t read or play them yet. Discs play from v0.2.0.
{{ c.label }}

Add your games

Add disc images you made from your own discs: a .cue with its .bin tracks, an .iso, an .m3u for a game on several discs, or a PS-EXE.

iStation reads your files where they are. It never copies, uploads or downloads games.
